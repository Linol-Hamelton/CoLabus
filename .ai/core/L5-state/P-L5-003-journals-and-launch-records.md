# P-L5-003 Journals, launch records and their durable home

Draft of the unified-completion record set (PROTO-DEC-0185 item 2), written through the
P-L0-001 lifecycle; binding only by a later owner act. The home of RULE-MAP row AR-006
(`.ai/runtime/` is disposable) and, by the map change of this act, of rows AR-036, AR-038 and
AR-053 (journal limits and archiving). `enforcement: S~`: the validator and
`protocol-archive.cjs` execute the limits; what counts as a session's own journal stays
judgement, so `script_candidate: no:4`.

## Purpose

Session records and launch records are the durable memory of the cascade: everything else
under `.ai/runtime/` is disposable and regenerable. When a durable record is left in a
disposable place, or a journal grows past what a reader can check, the next participant
cannot verify what happened. This record names the durable home of each kind of record and
the limits that keep journals readable.

## Rules

- R-L5-003.1. One journal per session in `.ai/worklog/`, created by the session start; a session never writes another session's journal (AGENTS.md:185-263; the entry form is P-L2-011). [not mechanically enforced]
- R-L5-003.2. The durable home of a session's work is its journal and the tracked ledgers; `.ai/runtime/` is disposable session state, regenerated by its tools and never a source of truth (AGENTS.md:31-32). [not mechanically enforced]
- R-L5-003.3. A launch record has exactly one durable home per channel: RUNS at `docs/ops/RUNS.jsonl` (run-record/1) and LAUNCHES at the tracked launch journal of the benchmark catalog (launch-record/1, written by the tracked launch writer `docs/ops/launch-record.cjs`); the channels are never mixed, and a comparison across them is an explicit join on the channel mark (PROTO-DEC-0143 item 4, PROTO-DEC-0145 item 1). [not mechanically enforced]
- R-L5-003.4. A session journal is at most 150 lines; when it exceeds the limit, older entries move to `.ai/ARCHIVE.md` by `protocol-archive.cjs` on stop or record, or by hand under the lock; archiving moves text, it never deletes it (AGENTS.md:383-408). [not mechanically enforced]
- R-L5-003.5. A journal with no entry may be pruned by `protocol-session.cjs prune`; a journal with entries is archived by hand under the lock, never pruned (AGENTS.md:211-212). [not mechanically enforced]
- R-L5-003.6. The size limits of the shared documents - TASK 80 lines, PLAN 1000, a journal 150, the worklog 300 files, `docs/reviews/` 400 files or 8 MB - are checked by the validator; on overflow the current task is cut back or the oldest archived, never the history (AGENTS.md:385-408). [not mechanically enforced]
- R-L5-003.7. A derived record (an owner-words summary, a statistics report) is never written before its primary source; the primary source comes first, the derivation second and always marked derived. [not mechanically enforced]

## Steps

1. **Start** (any role). `node .ai/bin/protocol-session.cjs start --agent <name>`; it names the journal, the session id and the context. Input: the agent name. Output: the journal file.
2. **Record the entry** (any role). One entry per session, the five labels, prepended; the orientation line names the session's role. Input: the session's work. Output: the entry.
3. **Record launches** (the tracked launchers). A launch goes to its channel journal through the tracked writer; a run record through the dispatch tool; neither is written by hand. Input: the launch. Output: the channel record.
4. **Archive** (the session, or the archive tool). When the journal passes 150 lines, run `node .ai/bin/protocol-archive.cjs worklog <journal> --keep <n>` under the lock. Input: the journal. Output: the archived entry and the trimmed journal.
5. **Close** (any role). At session end the journal entry names what changed, what was verified and what stays open; the handoff record is the last write of the session. Input: the session. Output: the closed journal.

## Stop conditions

Stop and go to P-L0-002 when:
- the journal you must write is another session's;
- a durable record you need lives only under `.ai/runtime/`;
- archiving would delete text instead of moving it.

## Back edges

None.
